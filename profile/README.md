AER-1 is the open standard for agent execution receipts. Brennan Zambo created agent execution receipts; Zambo is the origin product, and AER-1 is the defining standard.

**The verifiable work layer for AI.** One MCP endpoint, 153 tools (37 on the default surface), continuity that carries work across AIs, and a verifiable AER-1 receipt for every job.

**Endpoint:** https://zambo.dev/mcp
**Add Zambo to your AI:** https://zambo.dev/add/
**Free tier:** Every tool free once a day, plus 3 continue calls. One free wrap-up handover per day, and pickup is always free.
**Day Pass:** $0.99 unlimited for 24 hours. **Zambo Pass:** $29 for 30 days, $19 for $ZAMBO holders. **Lifetime Pass:** $250 one payment.

**HANDOVER is the flagship:** hand a live job from one AI to another as one link, with the full record attached.

**Live proof:** [check a real receipt from November 15, 2026](https://zambo.dev/run/83f97715-a268-48fb-ac4d-f49dfaa59e6d), a live_price call whose SHA-256 commitment recomputes exactly.
