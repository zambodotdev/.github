# Zambo: the verifiable work layer for AI

Zambo is the verifiable work layer for AI. One MCP endpoint, 153 tools (36 on the default surface), continuity that carries work across AIs, and a verifiable AER-1 receipt for every job.

**Endpoint:** https://zambo.dev/mcp
**Add Zambo to your AI:** https://zambo.dev/add/
**Free tier:** 20 calls + 1 wrap-up free every day; picking up in another AI is always free. Day Pass: $0.99 for unlimited use for 24 hours.

**Live proof:** [check a real receipt from November 7, 2026](https://zambo.dev/run/fe035cee-99bd-44e6-9cf6-881b851c5e71). Public receipts: 117,789 total, 3,449 issued on November 7, 2026. Live counts: https://zambo.dev/numbers/

Receipts follow AER-1 (IETF Internet-Draft draft-zambo-aer1-26; conformance corpus: 143 vectors, 146 checks). HANDOVER is the flagship: hand a live job from one AI to another as one link, with the full record attached.

Main repo: [zambo-mcp](https://github.com/zambodotdev/zambo-mcp). Founder: Brennan Zambo.
