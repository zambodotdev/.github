# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email brennanzambo@zambo.dev with the details: what you found, how to reproduce it, and the impact as you understand it. You will get an acknowledgment within 2 business days. Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability, and give the maintainer a reasonable window to ship a fix before any public disclosure.

## Scope

These repositories are the public, open-source surface of Zambo: the AER-1 conformance kit and verifiers, client packages, templates, and tools. The hosted service at zambo.dev is operated separately; reports about the hosted service are welcome at the same address.

## What a Zambo receipt proves

A verifiable AER-1 receipt proves the saved result was not changed since it was saved. It does not prove the result was right. Reports that rest on a receipt claiming more than that are documentation bugs: file them as regular issues with the exact wording.
